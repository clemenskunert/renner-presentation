--- PAGE 13 ---
List of selecting criteria for RENNER GP hammershanks
Most important, different design criterias to find the right RENNER GP hammershank:
▪ Flange type: 16 different flange types for repairs as standard range
▪ Wood type: hornbeam or maple
▪ Rolling length: short (like Steinway) or long for repairs
▪ Knuckle position: 17mm (standard) + 15,5mm
▪ Knuckle size: 10mm Standard + 9mm small diameter version in cutting width 11,7 mm
                                     and 10mm diameter in cutting width 11,2mm for repairs.
▪ Diameter: bass = 6,0mm, medium = 5,3mm, treble = 4,8mm
▪ Composition: 70 bass/ 20 treble or 60|10|20, 50|20|20, 75|25, 90, 100
