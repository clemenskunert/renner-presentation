First step of pre-voicing | Pre-needleing 
What to do?
▪ Pre- needleing in the fortissimo wedge
▪ Deep stitches to just before the underfelt
▪ As a result the hammerhead looses any tension, the tension is transferred to the forte wedge
▪ Therefore, this first step of pre-voicing is advisable in almost every case
Tools 
➢ Toning / voicing adjustable device for grand piano and upright hammerheads, Item number: 43-0005642
➢ Toning/voicing needle holder, professional type, with three needles, Item number: 43-0003270
➢ Voicing needle no.7 nickel plated Ø 0,60 mm bag with 25 pieces, Item number: 43-0003426
How to do?
▪ Clambing hammerheads (if should be done before gluing the hammerheads)
▪ Needle holder (voicing tool) with 2 or 3 needles, needle sizes 0,6- 0,8mm approx 9mm long
▪ Always work from the bottom up
▪ Do not stitch into underfelt or wooden core
▪ Your working zone is the fortissimo wedge, the areas between 9:00 and 10:00 and 2:00 and 3:00
▪ Bass 7-10 stiches horizontal
▪ Middle 5-10 stiches horizontal
▪ Treble 0-7 stiches vertical
▪ Pressing the needles is more effective and durable than hacking

Setting strings on frame pin
▪ Tuning is on 440 Hz or more
▪ Work from back to front, 
      frame pin > bridge pins > agrafe
▪ Carefully tap the string down onto 
      the pin
▪ Work with a brass chisel and light 
      hammer as not to damage the string

--- PAGE 17 ---
Setting strings on bridge pin
▪ Carefully tap the string down onto 
      the bridge pin
▪ First the rear, then the front pin
▪ Same tools as before
▪ Work carefully so that the string 
      does not press into the wood of 
      the bridge (this could lead to 
      noises)
▪ If unsure, knock on the inside
▪ Important: clearly defined string 
      supports
▪ The fuller the strings rest on
     their limit point, the more 
     powerfull is their sound
➢ Notice: the best hammerhead can only perform the function that was previously programmed into the instrument

String leveling
▪ Raising all strings and adjustment to a uniform level in agrafe (tis is not
the same as spreading the string after stringing)
▪ Finally align the string choirs in treble
▪ Be sure to perform when the instrument is on final tuning level

Check strike point and straightness of hammerheads
Check strike point of hammers in treble
▪ Check the strike point of note 88
▪ Slide keyframe in and out to find best spot
▪ The treble section of many pianos can be improved by re-setting the strike point from notes 69 through 88
▪ Correct if necessary
Check straightness of all hammerheads
▪ Can be done by pulling up with a hook and plucking
▪ Can be done by lifting the jack with finger and plucking
▪ Can be done by feeling and hearing with a light tapping
▪ To work effectively, mark with chalk on the key what needs correction

Mid- voicing | find uneven sections and balance
Mid-voicing
▪ Play up and down the keyboard chromatically
▪ Hear for sections, that have different characteristics, loudness 
or feeling
▪ Work out in green (forte) and orange (piano) wedge area if to hard
▪ This should help to create a balanced sound dynamic
▪ If you feel unsure how deep you can work prepare different tools 
with different needle length
▪ Be cautious but not fearful when stiching
▪ If a note or passage has become too soft, it can be corrected 
by lightly sanding
▪ Work out a balanced sound spectrum and volume level 
across the whole keyboard
▪ Accept the characteristics of the instrument

--- PAGE 23 ---
Final balance voicing
Compensate final balance
▪ Find single notes with an other character or loudness
▪ Work with a short single needle to compensate
▪ Work in piano (orange) and pianissimo (purple) wedge
▪ Remember the „red zone“ and do not work to deep at the crown!
▪ Play up and down the keyboard chromatically and listen for 
every uneven note
▪ Light shallow surface needling at or just under the crown will 
even out the offenders
▪ This is easily overdone, so proceed slowly
▪ Work between the string strokes, not directly inside
▪ Stich depth at the crown max. 2 mm
Tool tip:
➢ Toning/voicing needle holder BDK, solid brass, for 4 needles
      Item number: 43-0003269
➢ Toning/voicing needle holder, Chop Stick Item number: 43-0003271

Shift (left) padel voicing
Shift pedal voicing
▪ Finally, adjust the shift pedal correctly
▪ Play up and down the keyboard chromatically with padel 
pressed and listen on uniformity
▪ Work with particular care, as the main voicing may be affected
▪ Always work only on the strings directly affected
▪ Then connect with the main voicing by checking balance 
without the pedal
Expert tip: 
➢ Slightly breaking the left edge of the hammer crown with a 
fine sandpaper file can make this work much easier and can 
prevent noises
